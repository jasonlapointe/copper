**As of YYYY-MM-DD.** A few present-tense sentences: who they are to you, their current life,
the language and register they write in (formal vs. casual), and anything an interpreter must
know to make them sound like themselves. Copper refreshes this over time; days and weeks are
foregrounded, older detail fades.
